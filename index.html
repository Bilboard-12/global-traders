A local mill with a farming background

Global Traders began as a commercial farming company and moved into sunflower grain production. When we saw the market needed quality edible oil, we added value to our own crop by milling it.

With funding from the Uganda Development Bank, we bought our machinery in 2013 and started milling in 2015. Today we are one of the largest sunflower oil millers in the Acholi sub-region, with six full-time staff and about ten casual workers.

Sunflower is no longer our only line. We now clean simsim (sesame) to remove dust and waste, then weigh and pack it on our sesame seed packing unit, ready for the market. We are also preparing to start moringa oil production in 2027, so the mill can serve more farmers and more customers.

How MACO oil is made

Every batch follows the same steps, from the farmer's seed to a sealed jerrican. The oil is cold pressed and filtered.

  1. Receiving

    Our truck brings seed from farmer groups across Northern Uganda.

  2. Weighing

    Each delivery is weighed so farmers are paid for what they supply.

  3. Drying and cleaning

    Seed is sun dried, then cleaned on a vibro screen to remove dust and waste.

  4. Cold pressing

    The oil expeller presses the seed. Oil goes forward and the cake is set aside.

  5. Filtration

    A filter press clears the oil of fine particles.

  6. Packing

    Oil is packed and branded in 1 litre, 4 litre, 5 litre and 20 litre jerricans.

Affiliation and partnership policy

Global Traders Limited is open to working with private investors and other companies to achieve excellence and increase production. We believe good partnerships grow the whole sector: more seed bought from farmers, more oil pressed, and more quality products reaching customers.

Private investors

We welcome investors who want to back our milling, packing and expansion plans, including new products such as moringa oil.

Other companies

We work with processors, traders, distributors, buyers and service providers who share our standards and want to grow with us.

Our shared goal

To raise quality, increase production and build a stronger sunflower and oilseed value chain in Northern Uganda.

  1. Shared goals. We affiliate with partners whose aims match ours: excellence in quality and higher, steadier production.
  2. Quality first. Every partnership must protect the quality, safety and good name of MACO products.
  3. Honest dealings. We keep agreements clear and fair, and we expect open communication and timely payment from every party.
  4. Fair to farmers. Any growth must keep farmer groups, who supply our seed, fairly treated and fairly paid.
  5. Lawful and ethical. All partners must follow Ugandan law and the standards set by the authorities that regulate our business.
  6. Written agreements. Terms, roles, investment and returns are agreed in writing before any partnership begins.
  7. Review. Partnerships are reviewed regularly so both sides keep gaining and can adjust as production grows.

Who we supply

We sell retail and wholesale to anyone who wants our products, in three ways.

  • At the factory. Collect oil or sunflower cake directly from our mill in Gulu.
  • Bulk delivery. Bulk buyers order and we transport the oil to their location.
  • Market days. We sell at markets across the region on their market days.
  • Our customers include schools, bakeries, retailers and hospitals.

A training ground for students

We work with universities, especially Gulu University, to give students hands-on skills in agricultural practice, food processing, agri-business and management. Our operations also give regular work to people in the communities around the factory.
